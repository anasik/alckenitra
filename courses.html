Info about our

Courses

ALC Kenitra offers six sessions throughout the year, from early September to the end of July.
ALC Kenitra is closed during the month of August.

CHILDREN (Ages 7-10)

Children's courses are 60 hours and available during Fall, Winter, and Spring sessions (20 hours each).

Schedule:

  • Wednesday: 14h00-16h00, 16h10-18h10
  • Saturday: 9h00-11h00, 11h10-13h10

Course Levels:

  • Child One
  • Child Two
  • Child Three
  • Child Four
  • Child Five

JUNIORS COURSES (Ages 11-14)

Courses for Juniors are 60 hours and available during the following combinations:

  • Fall + Winter + Spring (20 hours each session)
  • Winter + Spring (30 hours each session)
  • June Intensive + July Intensive (30 hours each session, limited availability)

Juniors Winter Intensive (January to June):

  • Wednesday: 14h30-17h30
  • Junior Starter
  • Junior One
  • Junior Two
  • Junior Three
  • Junior Four
  • Junior Five

ADULT COURSES (Ages 15 and above)

Courses for Adults offer 30 hours of instruction and are available during three regular sessions (Fall, Winter, Spring) and three intensive sessions (June, July, September) per year.

Regular Session Class Times:

  • Monday/Wednesday: 18h40-20h10 (1.5 hours)
  • Tuesday/Thursday: 18h40-20h10 (1.5 hours)
  • Friday: 15h30-18h30 (3 hours)
  • Saturday: 15h00-18h00 (3 hours)

BACCALAUREATE CLASSES

Baccalaureate Classes - Every January

  • Fridays: 18h30-21h30

Duration and Cost:

  • 48 hours - 2000 DH

CONVERSATION CLASSES

Conversation Class - April 2026

Schedule and Duration:

  • Fridays: 18h30-21h30
  • 30 hours total - 1200 DH

This class focuses on having normal conversations and interacting with a variety of topics. Students have the opportunity to build and develop fluency, improve their accent, and learn communication skills in a more informal and relaxed environment.

PRIVATE CLASSES

Availability and Pricing:

  • Individual or small group instruction is available at any time
  • Pricing: 300 DH per hour (≤10 hours), 250 DH per hour (>10 hours)
