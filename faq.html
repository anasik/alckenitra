Frequently Asked Questions

What are your registration fees?

Fees vary depending on the course you register for. Have a look at our Fees page for costs.

When is the next session?

Have a look at our Upcoming Session page!

When are your classes?

We offer classes afternoons and evenings during the week, and on Saturdays throughout the day. There are no classes on Sundays.

What is the deadline to register?

Registration opens about one month before the session starts and continues through the second week of the session. See our Upcoming Session page for more information on dates.

How do I register?

Stop by the ALC during the registration period during our office hours. For specific registration requirements, see our Registration page.

What is the minimum age to register?

Kids: 7-10 years old; Juniors: 11-14 years old; Adults: 15 years old and up.

Do you offer private classes? How much are they?

Yes, we do! See our Fees page for price details.

Do you have online classes?

ALC offers private online classes for individuals or groups.

Do you teach French, German, or other languages besides English?

We only specialize in the English Language. We do not teach French, German, or other languages besides English.

Can I get a certificate after I finish all my classes?

Certificates are given upon request to any student who successfully completes the course. For fee information, see our Fees page.

How can I get my final exam result?

Final exam results are posted at the centre.

I missed the final exam, can I take it? Do I have to pay?

Under certain circumstances we can arrange a make-up test. See our Fees page for details on applicable fees.

Can I change my class time? Do I have to pay?

Course time can be changed, depending on availability. There is a fee for any change. See our Fees page for details.

I stopped my classes last year. Do I have to take a placement test to start again?

If you missed three or more sessions you must take a placement test prior to registering again.

Do you offer scholarships? How can I apply?

At the present time we do not offer scholarships.

Can I take the TOEFL or TOEIC exam at ALC? How much is it?

ALC Kenitra does not offer TOEFL or TOEIC exams.

Do you have a TOEIC Preparation course?

No, we do not.

Do you have an IELTS Preparation course?

No, we do not.

Do you have a CELTA program?

ALC Kenitra does not have a training program. However, ALC Kenitra offers the English Teaching Internship Initiative (ETII) for Master's students and BA students in English wishing to become English teachers. See our ETII page for more information.

How can I join a club?

See our clubs page and click on the club that interests you. Then contact the club advisor to ask about joining. You can also reach out to o.bziker.kenitra@acamorocco.org for club information.
