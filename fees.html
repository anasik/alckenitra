Fees

Fees, Refunds, and Cancellation

Tuition and other fees are payable on site by check, cash, or bank card. It is also possible to pay by wire transfer.

Fees do not include the cost of textbooks.

Children (ages 7-11) Juniors (ages 12-14) Adults (age 15 and above)
Children's courses run for 60 hours (one year) and the fee is 3000 DH All juniors courses run for 60 hours (one year) and the fee is 3000 DH All regular classes are 30 hours per session and the fee is 1200 DH

Frais, Remboursements et Annulation

Les frais sont payables sur place par chèque, en espèces ou par carte bancaire. Il est aussi possible de régler par virement bancaire.

Les frais ne couvrent pas le prix des livres.
