About Us

ALC Kenitra

The American Language Center of Kénitra was founded in 1988. Since that time our enrollment has grown to nearly 1600 students per session. We offer English classes for adults, juniors (12-14 years of age), and children (7-10 years of age). Open to our students are non-classroom activities and resources such as a lending library, clubs, cultural events, and workshops. We also have an active community service club that serves Kenitra and the surrounding areas.

Location: 2 Boulevard El Qadissia, Kenitra 14000, Morocco (across from the new train station)

About ALC Kenitra

In addition to studying English, students can participate in many extracurricular activities, such as joining a club (See our clubs page for more information).

Our staff and teachers

ALC English instructors are Moroccan and also native English speakers. All are professionally trained and certified with years of classroom experience.
