Cultural Events

Cultural Events bring the ALC community together through performances, exhibitions, film screenings, and celebrations that highlight Moroccan and international cultures.

These events give students real-world chances to practice English while connecting with artists, speakers, and guest performers.

Events are announced throughout the term and are open to students, alumni, and friends of ALC.

For announcements and participation, contact
alckenitracontact@acamorocco.org.
